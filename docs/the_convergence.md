# THE CONVERGENCE — the Saga's spine

> ## The Saga is called **THE RISE**
> **Named 2026-07-26 (user):** *"it chronicles the awkward rise of the Galeans onto
> the universe's grand stage."*
>
> It is ironic at both ends without lying at either. At hour one it reads as plain
> triumph — a scrappy pilot in a junk hull, a people finding their feet. By the end
> it means you rose by breaking open a prison, and the win condition is literally to
> **ascend** and earn a stay of execution. It also sits against **ADO'VIETES —
> "The Idiots"** (§ below), which is what the elders call the same event.
>
> **And it is safe to print.** "The Convergence" and "The Idiot" were both
> considered and rejected as the player-facing name: each states the late reveal, so
> either would hand the player the shape of the ending in their first hour. "The
> Rise" is about the Galeans, not the threat — it gives nothing away and gains its
> weight in hindsight.
>

The spine of the whole game (and the series). Everything else — the gates, the
Cinderweb, the Wardens, the Threshers, the factions, the professions — hangs off
this. Set by the user 2026-07-22. Read this before writing any Campaign beat.

## One line
Humanity, flying gate to gate in ignorance, has been unlocking a prison — and the
demons inside are converging. The species is now on trial for its own recklessness,
and must unite against three fronts to prove it deserves to exist.

## The premise, in order

1. **The gates were a PRISON.** The Wardens (elder alien gate-keepers) built the
   gate network to hold the LEVIATHANS — the cosmic horrors — apart from one
   another. Separated, each is a contained terror. **Converged, they are the end
   of everything.** The gates are cell doors. **Cinderweb is one of the prisoners.**

2. **Humanity is the crime.** We found the gates and flew through them, gate to
   gate, thinking we were exploring / escaping / ascending. We were breaking the
   locks. We freed the convergence out of pure ignorance — too young, too
   reckless, too blind to understand what we were opening. **The player has been
   committing the crime the entire game** (gate travel is the core mechanic; the
   starter Campaign *ends* on opening the WayGate — the "triumph" is the
   catastrophe).

3. **The Wardens are RIGHT.** They are not villains. They are executioners
   carrying out a proportionate sentence: humanity released an extinction event
   and is too dangerous and ignorant to be allowed to continue. Their hostility
   is JUSTIFIED and TRAGIC. This is the Saga's moral engine — you do not fight an
   evil, you fight a verdict you earned.

4. **Three fronts.** Humanity now faces annihilation on three sides:
   - **The Convergence** — the Leviathans (Cinderweb + its kin), freed and
     drawing together. The apocalypse itself.
   - **The Wardens** — carrying out the death sentence. The judge and executioner.
   - **The Threshers** — the reaping tide (System 3 space-bugs), casual and cold.
   Three distinct terrors, three player verbs: **flee** the horror, **outplay**
   the Wardens, **don't be in the field** when the Threshers come.

5. **The trial — UNITE OR DIE.** Humanity is fractured (pirates / guardians /
   colonists / rival commissions). The only path is to UNITE — the XCOM
   cross-faction research and alliance is the *mechanical expression of the theme*.
   Ruel and Krayt cooperating is humanity passing the test.

6. **The win is MORAL, not military.** You cannot kill your way out. Victory =
   **STOP THE CONVERGENCE** — clean up the mess we made — and by doing so, ASCEND:
   prove to the Wardens that humanity can grow past the recklessness it was
   condemned for. Earn the stay of execution. A species coming of age.

7. **THE REVEAL — "we're the bad guys."** The pivot of the whole game. The
   Wardens have been transmitting the SAME message since first contact, in a
   language humanity can't read — hostile static, untranslated glyphs. Only when
   the player has REVERSE-ENGINEERED enough Warden tech (the XCOM research) do
   they finally decode it: *"You are guilty of breaching four prison gates.
   Convergence is nigh inevitable. You are guilty of genocide and scheduled for
   extinction. Desist immediately."* The player realizes they have been the
   ANTAGONIST of the backstory the entire time — and that every "battle" was a
   plea they couldn't hear. They stop opening gates. **The information isn't
   dropped on the player; they EARN the ability to understand what they already
   saw**, and re-read their own history as tragedy.
   - MUTUAL reveal: the Wardens execute humanity because they assumed we were
     DHAKAR COLLABORATORS (who else deliberately breaks four locks?). Decoding
     works both ways — we learn we are guilty; they learn we are merely IGNORANT.
     That symmetry is what unlocks the alliance: not forgiveness, but the horror
     of a war both sides fought by mistake while the real enemy moved in the dark.

8. **THE DHAKAR — the true final enemy** (working name; alts: the Sundered, the
   Umbral, the Vhakar). The DARK MIRROR of the Wardens: as advanced, same
   crystalline space-folding tech, but their SWORN ENEMIES and opposite soul.
   Where the Wardens are the JAILERS, the Dhakar are whatever they SPLIT FROM —
   the faction that wants the Leviathans FREE. They arrive around the reveal and
   move to FINISH opening the gates and FORCE the Convergence. The player flips
   from the one who broke the locks to the one DEFENDING the last of them.
   - LOOK: Wraithlight INVERTED. Black crystal / obsidian; light DRUNK rather than
     emitted (cold violet, or a glow that reads as a wound). Same crystalline TECH
     as the Wardens but the opposite POSTURE: where a Warden hull is a graceful
     crescent/heron-blade (serene, mourning), a Dhakar hull is a **CLAW** — dark,
     sharp, hooked, barbed, all edge and intent. A Warden ship looks like it is
     grieving; a Dhakar ship looks like it is REACHING FOR YOU. Concept art:
     docs/concepts/dhakar/.
   - THE DHAKAR THEMSELVES — ROBED VULTURE-ASSASSINS (portrait LOCKED 2026-07-24,
     user): where the ships are CLAWS, the beings are hooded, gaunt figures draped
     in tattered dark robes; the head is a gnarled vulture-visage with a long
     twisted hooked beak; deep-set eyes burn cold VIOLET, leaking from the sockets
     like a wound. Carrion-patient killers — death that circles. The exact dark
     mirror of the Azitaire's radiant crystal-HOUND grace (threats_wardens.md): one
     wears light, the other wears robes; one is a noble beast of gold, the other a
     beaked scavenger of violet rot. Portrait: docs/concepts/dhakar/dhakar_portrait.png.
   - THEIR MOTIVE — HUBRIS, NOT NIHILISM. They believe they can TRAP the
     Leviathans at the instant of Convergence and HARNESS the dark matter for
     INFINITE ENERGY. It is all POWER to them — to fuel their society and their
     weapons. If it works, they are infinite. Not a death-cult; power junkies
     gambling all of existence on a theory that you can cage a god at its birth
     and drink it forever. Hubris with a spreadsheet.
   - THE TWO-OUTCOME HORROR (why "stop them, period" is the only sane response):
     there is NO outcome where the Convergence completing is survivable.
     - **They are WRONG → EXTINCTION.** The Leviathans merge; everything ends.
     - **They are RIGHT → something WORSE.** They become infinite — eternal,
       unassailable, cruel masters of a caged universe forever. No death, no
       escape, no end. Best case is oblivion.
     And NOBODY KNOWS which: the Dhakar believe, the Wardens will not risk finding
     out. You cannot allow the experiment to run, because the experiment is
     existence itself.

9. **The win, and the horizon.** Stop the Dhakar from completing the Convergence
   (defend the last gate / destroy their convergence-engine — mechanic TBD).
   Succeed and humanity earns SURVIVAL and a RETICENT WARDEN ALLY — they will
   never like us, but they will stand with us, because now they understand. The
   sequel sees humanity AND Wardens together against new threats (and the Dhakar's
   unanswered "why"). The trial's reward is a future.

## The elder name for humanity — ADO'VIETES ("The Idiots")
To the Wardens and the Dhakar — ancient beyond language — humanity has no proper
name. We are **Ado'vietes**: *the idiots*. Not a threat-designation, not even
contempt with heat in it — a filing category. The idiots who wandered into the
archive and started pulling gates open until the wing collapsed. Every decoded
Warden transmission calls us this; it is the drumbeat of the elders' judgment.

**THE REDEMPTION IS TONAL, NOT LINGUISTIC.** They NEVER stop calling us the
idiots — because we are, gloriously, idiots, and pretending otherwise would be a
lie. The word does not change. Its TEMPERATURE does. For the whole game
*Ado'vietes* is said with cold contempt (a filing category). The endgame beat:
the shooting stops, and an ancient, immortal, infinitely superior Warden looks at
these ridiculous short-lived creatures who — against all sense and all odds —
turned and did the RIGHT thing, and just... exhales. Shakes its head. The
smallest smile. *"...you idiots."* And means it now the way you mean it about a
friend who is a beautiful disaster.

That is the difference between RESPECT and LOVE, and this Saga chose love. We do
not prove we are NOT idiots (we can't; we opened four prison gates because they
were *there*). We prove that idiots can be worth standing beside anyway. The
Wardens do not knight us — they ADOPT us. Exasperated, fond, permanent. The trial
was never "become something other than what you are." It was "be SEEN — every
reckless, in-over-its-head inch — and accepted whole."

## The theme, now that the villain is complete: POWER vs RESTRAINT
The Dhakar's motive reveals what the whole Saga is *about*. The gates were never
just a prison — they are a **FIREWALL**: the Wardens keep the Leviathans APART
specifically to deny the Dhakar their fuel. Every lock is a "no" to their sundered
kin's gamble, held for eons. Then the *Ado'vietes* wandered in and began opening
it — **doing the Dhakar's work by accident.** (This is why the Wardens assumed
COLLABORATION and marked us for extinction: from where they stand, breaking those
gates *is* the enemy's entire plan.)

And the Dhakar are **humanity's dark future** — what an advanced species becomes if
it never learns the word *enough*: power without restraint, curiosity curdled into
appetite. So humanity's trial is not only "will you survive?" but "**are you
Dhakar-in-embryo, or something better?**" We broke the gates out of ignorant
reaching; they would break the last one out of calculated greed. Stopping them is
humanity REJECTING what it could grow into — choosing existence over power. THAT
is what turns *Ado'vietes* from a slur into an endearment (see above): the Wardens
accept us not because we stopped being idiots, but because — offered the exact
temptation that made the Dhakar — the idiots said **no**. Same word, said with a
smile now.

## How it recontextualizes what already exists
- **Gate travel** (core mechanic) = the crime, ongoing. Every gate = a lock (and
  a firewall against the Dhakar's gamble).
- **The WayGate** ("The Ancient Gate", unknown origin) = a Warden prison-lock.
  The starter Campaign's climactic gate-opening is the inciting catastrophe,
  reframed as dramatic irony after the reveal.
- **Cinderweb** = one converging Leviathan, not a local monster. Its "cannot be
  killed in open play, retreats and returns" rule becomes LORE: the beast was
  never the problem, the convergence is — you can only drive it off until you
  address the root.
- **The traceless disappearances / "Nothing Left Behind" / Voss's "no debris"** =
  the convergence beginning, a freed Leviathan feeding.
- **The Warden probes that watch then leave** (foreshadow-then-contact) = the
  keepers observing the species that just opened a cell, deciding its sentence.
- **Micro-Warp / blink / gates** = scavenged Warden space-folding — humanity
  fumbling with the keys to the prison it doesn't know it broke.
- **XCOM cross-faction research** = unite-or-die, the theme as a mechanic.

## Open questions (resolve when building the mid/late Saga)
- **The REVEAL moment.** When/how does the player learn the gates were a prison
  and they're the arsonist? Likely Warden first contact (System 2-3). It should
  land as a gut-punch that recontextualizes the player's own past actions.
- **"Stop the convergence" — the mechanic.** What does the win condition actually
  ask the player to DO? (Re-seal gates? A convergence-point set-piece? Forge a
  Warden-tech weapon via the cross-faction research? TBD.)
- **Tone of the System 1 ending.** The demo-end already isn't a clean triumph
  (Krayt devoured, the truce a grief-heal). A faint seed of the coming unease in
  the ThankYou screen would pay off the reveal without spoiling it.
- **Can a Leviathan ever be truly killed?** The mortality rule says only at a
  system finale (`mortal`). Ties to "stopping the convergence" — killing one may
  require severing it from the convergence first.
