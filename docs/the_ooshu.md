# The Ooshu — Masked Trackers-for-Hire

Design doc for the **Ooshu**, a neutral alien race of hunters and finders. First
written canon 2026-07-24 (user). Debuts in the Cinder Reach Campaign ("The Legend",
`the-legend`) as an Ooshu bounty hunter tracking Conall "Starfall" Phelan.

## Tier — the NEUTRAL third party (for hire, or hunting you)
Not a great power (Galean / Quarn) and not beyond-human (Azitaire / Dhakar /
Cinderweb / Thresher). The Ooshu stand OUTSIDE the wars: a nomadic people
renowned as the galaxy's **trackers, finders, and bounty hunters**, loyal only to
the contract. You can be hunted by one, or (later) hire one. Grounded and mortal —
they bleed, they can be beaten — but patient, professional, and unnervingly hard
to shake.

## The mask — TWO-FOLD (LOCKED 2026-07-24, user)
Every Ooshu in Galean space wears a heavy multi-lens respirator and scavenger
wraps. It serves two purposes at once:

1. **DISGUISE.** It hides *what* they are. To a Galean concourse an Ooshu just
   reads as some scarred, private mercenary in a rebreather — nobody clocks it as
   ALIEN. This banks a reveal: the "it was never human" beat can land whenever the
   story wants it, because the pilot (and the player) simply assumed a masked merc.
2. **LIFE-SUPPORT.** It is a genuine **rebreather** — necessary, not cosmetic (see
   below). An Ooshu cannot survive unmasked in a Galean atmosphere.

Portrait concept: `docs/concepts/ooshu/ooshu_bounty_hunter.png`.

## Why the mask is NECESSARY — nitrogen, the fatal affinity (LOCKED 2026-07-24, user)
Not a toxin — an **affinity**. The Ooshu's blood-analog binds **nitrogen** far
more avidly than the respiratory gas they actually live on — the exact mechanism
by which **carbon monoxide** locks onto human hemoglobin and suffocates us while
oxygen is right there. "Not technically poisonous — their bodies just love it too
much" (user).

Consequences that make it load-bearing, not trivia:
- **A Galean atmosphere is ~78% nitrogen.** An unmasked Ooshu doesn't dramatically
  *poison* and thrash — they QUIETLY SUFFOCATE, surrounded by air, their own blood
  saturating with the gas it can't stop grabbing. Eerie and slow.
- **The mask is a rebreather**, scrubbing nitrogen and feeding their real breathing
  mix — so a **breached mask is a death clock.** Crack an Ooshu's rebreather in
  Galean air and the relentless hunter becomes the one running out of time. A
  built-in vulnerability and a set-piece.
- **The whole inhabited galaxy is hostile air to them** — everywhere Galeans live,
  an Ooshu can only visit *sealed*. Explains the wraps, the detachment, the
  mercenary remove: they are always, everywhere, a guest holding their breath.

## Story role — Conall's hunter ("The Legend")
Someone put a price on **Starfall** (open: the Quarn? a Galean faction that wants
the war restarted? — see the_quarn.md for why finding Conall reignites it), and an
Ooshu took the contract. A film-noir antagonist who keeps CLOSING IN on Conall
through the campaign — patient, faceless, professional. The mask-breach
vulnerability is the seam for how that hunt can finally turn.

## PROFESSION SEAM — the Bounty Hunter (banked, not built)
The Ooshu open a SEVENTH commission when the time comes (design 2026-07-24, user).
EverQuest touchstone: the **RANGER** — whose irreplaceable claim was TRACKING.
- **ROLE: Damage + single-target CONTROL.** NOT Support, NOT Healing. (User's
  taxonomy: *Healing* is its own thing; *Support* = buff allies / debuff enemies;
  *Control* = lockdown. A lone hunter neither mends nor buffs a party — they pin
  prey.) "Control" here is single-target LOCKDOWN, distinct from Miner's AREA
  Tangle.
- **SIGNATURE — THE MARK:** tag one target and it cannot get away — you see it
  through cloak / dark / distance (tracking), and it can't out-run or shake your
  lock. Control over the prey's OPTIONS (flee, hide, escape), NOT a damage-amp
  debuff (that would be Support).
- **DAMAGE:** burst-vs-the-mark; the hunt ends in a takedown — or a **CAPTURE**
  (bounties pay more alive: subdue to low hull, then tag; ties to the mask-breach
  vulnerability and the existing bounty board / MissionLog).
- **COOP value is CONTROL, not a buff:** the BH LOCKS the mark down and REVEALS it
  to the party ("it can't run — converge"), rather than amping team damage.
- **LEADER is NOT the introductory hunter.** The hunter is first CONTACT
  (foreshadow-then-contact, like the Wardens); the commission LEADER is a LATER
  Cinder Reach Campaign reveal (TBD) — earned by besting/surviving the hunter, or
  by chasing who put the price on Starfall.
