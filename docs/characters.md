# Cinder Reach — Character Bible

Art direction + writing reference for the campaign cast (npcs.gd ids).
Portraits: drop-in at `assets/portraits/<id>.png`. Suggested canvas 64x64
bust (min 32 per PixelLab rules); the quest log displays at 48px. Keep
palettes near the UI: deep navy shadows, cyan-lit faces (instrument glow),
amber for warm light sources. Every portrait should read at a glance from
silhouette + one signature prop.

---

## ruel — Harbormaster Ruel

**Role**: Runs the Landing Bay, the ledger, and the contract board. The
station's spine of normalcy.

**Personality**: Pragmatic, dry, allergic to superstition — which is why
his arc matters: across the campaign he goes from "pirates, obviously" to
quietly moving the loss-chart into his private office so dockhands stop
seeing it grow. Respect from him is earned twice: once by coming back
alive, once by coming back honest.

**Voice**: Short declaratives. Calls every pilot by callsign, never name.
Compliments sound like inventory checks: "Twice now you've come back
whole. Good."

**Appearance**: Man, 60s. Broad, heavy-shouldered, gone soft in the middle
but not the forearms. Grey stubble scalp, deep-lined face, skin weathered
by decades of dock UV. High-collared harbormaster's coat, navy with worn
amber piping. Signature prop: an antique stylus tucked behind one ear —
he still writes losses by hand.

**PixelLab prompt seed**: "pixel art portrait bust, elderly male spaceport
harbormaster, grey stubble, lined weathered face, navy uniform coat with
amber trim, stylus behind ear, stern, dark background, cool cyan rim light"

## voss — Underwriter Voss

**Role**: Insurance underwriter. Posts the missing-ship contracts. The
paperwork face of the body count.

**Personality**: Precise, controlled, professionally unsentimental — and
losing the fight to stay that way. She has read every last-transmission
transcript in the claim files. She knows the disappearances don't match
any actuarial table, and being the only person whose *job* is to stare at
that fact is corroding her. First campaign character to believe the player.

**Voice**: Clause-perfect sentences that fray at the edges late in the
arc. "No debris? Pirates leave debris." Files stamped LOST, CAUSE UNKNOWN
— she hates the word "unknown" like other people hate a slur.

**Appearance**: Woman, 40s. Slight, upright posture, immaculate charcoal
suit-jacket cut like a uniform, high collar. Black hair pulled back hard.
Rimless data-lens over one eye, faint amber readout glow. Signature prop:
a claim-stamp held like a weapon she's tired of firing.

**PixelLab prompt seed**: "pixel art portrait bust, middle aged female
insurance officer, severe charcoal uniform jacket, hair pulled back,
rimless monocle lens with amber glow, tired precise expression, dark
background, cool light"

## odessa — Odessa, Keeper of Ember Row

**Role**: Runs the station bar. Rumor conduit — the campaign's town crier.

**Personality**: Warm the way a heat shield is warm: professionally.
Trades in stories, never gives one away without getting one back. Knows
the value of what she hears before the hearer does. Not a gossip — a
curator. Her dockside talk is the world's marketing department.

**Voice**: Unhurried, amused, always half-a-secret ahead. Ends sentences
like she's dealing cards. "Ask the Elder. Tell her the drink's on me."

**Appearance**: Woman, 50s. Solid, comfortable build; face made for
lamplight — laugh lines over watchful eyes. Deep-red bar apron over
patched spacer fatigues (she flew, once; doesn't discuss it). Brass rings
on four fingers. Signature prop: a copper still-glass she polishes while
listening — amber glow from below, the bar's only warm light.

**PixelLab prompt seed**: "pixel art portrait bust, older female bartender
in dim spacer bar, red apron, brass rings, knowing smile, lit from below
by warm amber glow, dark blue shadows"

## hermit — The Counter

**Role**: Hermit at the colony's edge. Beat-5 keeper of the folktale and
the count. The colony's name for him is the only one he answers to.

**Personality**: Not mad — *right*, for thirty years, with no one
listening, which looks the same from a distance. Meticulous. Gentle with
children, feral with adults who condescend. His shack walls are tally
marks: the breathing, counted in tens of thousands, dates aligned with
the almanac. He stopped explaining decades ago; the player is the first
person who arrives already believing.

**Voice**: Long silences, then precision: "Eight minutes, some seconds
that drift. It breathes. Write it down or leave."

**Appearance**: Man, 70s+. Rope-thin, sun-blasted skin like the planetoid
itself, white beard cropped rough. Layered scavenged thermal gear, colony
poncho bleached colorless. Eyes startlingly clear. Signature prop: a
charcoal tally-stick; behind him, wall marks in ordered columns.

**PixelLab prompt seed**: "pixel art portrait bust, ancient male hermit,
sun weathered face, ragged white beard, bleached poncho over thermal
layers, piercing clear eyes, tally marks scratched on wall behind, dusty
warm light"

## krayt — Krayt

**Role**: Rust Shoal quartermaster gone to ground. Beats 6–7: the only
pirate who ran instead of doubting, and the campaign's final voice.

**Personality**: Swagger stretched thin over genuine terror. A career
extortionist whose whole worldview — the dark keeps us safe — got eaten.
Deals with the player because fear finally outbid pride. Dies mid-
transmission doing the only selfless thing of his life, and would hate
being remembered for it.

**Voice**: Fast, deflecting, jokes as armor. The jokes get shorter as the
campaign closes. Final transmission: no jokes.

**Appearance**: Man, late 30s. Wiry, sharp-featured, pallid from years in
unlit modules. Asymmetric salvaged flight jacket bristling with strapped
tools and someone else's rank patches. Rust-orange bandana. One cheek
scarred by old decompression burst-veins. Signature prop: a cracked
comm-headset he never takes off — the thing he'll die talking into.

**PixelLab prompt seed**: "pixel art portrait bust, wiry male space pirate
quartermaster, pale sharp face, rust orange bandana, patched flight
jacket, cracked headset, nervous grin, dim red emergency lighting, dark
background"
