# The Quarn — Living Doom Machines

Design doc for the **Quarn**, the Cinder Reach's grounded war-rival. First written
canon 2026-07-24 (user) — before today the Quarn were named in the campaign lore
(CLAUDE.md, `the-legend`) but had ZERO design. This locks who and what they are.

## Tier — the geopolitical peer-power (NOT cosmic, elder, or swarm)
The game's threat roster keeps each antagonist a different *kind* of "beyond
Galean":

| Threat | Nature | Player verb |
|---|---|---|
| Human pirates | mundane, greedy | **outgrow** |
| Cinderweb / leviathans | cosmic horror, unknowable | **flee** |
| The Azitaire (Wardens) | ancient, precise elders | **outplay** |
| **The Quarn** | **a rival great power — a WAR you fight** | **hold the line / win the war** |

The Quarn are the one enemy you fight a *conventional war* with: fleets, cruisers,
a casus belli, a cold standoff that can reignite. They are relatable as an
adversary the way the others never are — an empire, not a mystery.

## Identity — LIVING DOOM MACHINES (LOCKED 2026-07-24, user)
Not soldiers in armor — **the armor IS the Quarn.** A Quarn is a heavy, living
war-machine animated by **caged lightning**: cold blue-white electricity sealed
inside dark segmented battle-plating, arcing from the seams, joints, and visor.
No pilot, no person underneath; the lightning is what makes the machine *live* and
*kill*. "Living doom machines" — that's the read (user's phrase). Heavy, slow-
seeming, industrial, disciplined, and utterly lethal. A war-body barely keeping
its own power caged.

- **Palette:** dark gunmetal / black plating / **cold blue-white lightning** /
  electric-blue eye-glow.
- Portrait concept: `docs/concepts/quarn/quarn_portrait.png`.

### The thematic rhyme — "lightning in a bottle" (user)
The Quarn and the **Azitaire** (threats_wardens.md) are BOTH energy caged in a
vessel — but OPPOSITE souls:
- **Azitaire** = WARM GOLD light held CALM within noble crystal — grace at rest.
- **Quarn** = COLD VIOLENT lightning caged in war-plating — a weapon straining its
  bottle.

Same idea, worlds apart in feel. It's a deliberate galaxy-wide rhyme, not a
coincidence — and it keeps two "energy" races from ever being confused.

## Story role — the war, and STARFALL
The Quarn were **ADVANCING** — a war-push toward Galean space — and their advance
was **HALTED BY STARFALL** (Conall "Starfall" Phelan, the planet hermit / "The
Counter"). His legendary act — splashing a Quarn cruiser to save Percival — is
what stopped them and froze the front into an uneasy cold standoff.

That is why Conall is a **hunted living casus belli**: the peace holds only while
the legend stays buried. Reveal that Starfall lives and the Quarn advance
resumes — the Galean/Quarn war reignites. The Cinder Reach Campaign ("The Legend",
`the-legend`) is film-noir built on exactly this: keep the legend dead; when it
fails, Conall becomes the legend again and, at the finale, **draws the Quarn INTO
the gate** to save the Reach — braiding the Campaign into the Saga's one gate
set-piece. Conall becomes a Saga-spanning thread who dies far later ending the
Quarn fleet.

## Braid — MILDLY to the Wardens, NEVER connected (canon, CLAUDE.md)
The Quarn are the *geopolitical-war* alien tier, only faintly braided with the
Azitaire/Wardens and never revealed to be connected. They are a mortal-scale
empire; the elder war (Azitaire / Dhakar / the Convergence) is a different order
of thing entirely. Keep them separate.

## Mechanics — NOT BUILT (design notes)
- **Damage flavour:** slots into the four-threat wheel (threats_wardens.md) as the
  human/ballistic tier's *heavier* cousin — massed, disciplined firepower and
  armor. (Distinct from Warden clean-energy, Cinderweb bio-devour, Thresher
  corrode.) Exact kit TBD with the fleet work.
- **Tempo:** heavy and inexorable, not agile — you hold a line against them, you
  don't dogfight them one-on-one. A Quarn cruiser is a wall.
- Build seam: reuses the fleet/super-heavy hull work from `the-legend`; the
  "living machine, no pilot" fiction means a Quarn wreck is just dead metal (no
  escape pod, no body) — a clean contrast to human wrecks.
